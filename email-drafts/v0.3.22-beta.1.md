---
headline: threadfork 0.3.22-beta.1 hardens IPC security and fixes llama port conflicts
subhead: IPC peer verification, deep-link validation, and sidecar port isolation to prevent interference with other tools.
preheader: Security fixes and llama sidecar improvements.
---

- **IPC peer credentials now verified.** threadfork validates that inter-process messages come from its own processes and rate-limits setup events to prevent abuse.
- **Deep-link callbacks validated before session injection.** OAuth callback shape and token claims are checked; malformed or tampered callbacks are rejected.
- **llama sidecar no longer claims ports in use.** Fixed a conflict where the local inference engine could block ports needed by other applications.
- **Webview filesystem scope narrowed and note asset refs validated.** Tightened sandboxing to prevent access outside the notes directory.

Stay secure — threadfork team
